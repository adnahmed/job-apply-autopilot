# Eligibility Policy V5.11 — Decisive Evidence

Derive the candidate's home country and configured location lanes from `profile.yaml`; do not hardcode them in policy. Submit only with reasonable positive evidence that the exact role can employ the candidate in that jurisdiction or through an explicit bridge.

Positive:
- exact candidate home-country job location;
- candidate home country explicitly included in eligible countries;
- explicit worldwide/international hiring;
- explicit Asia/APAC/APJ scope with no conflicting exclusion;
- explicit global contractor arrangement;
- explicit sponsorship/relocation/international mobility bridge;
- verified employing entity in the candidate's home country tied to this role.

Not sufficient alone:
- `Remote`;
- LinkedIn worldwide search placement;
- global company/team;
- candidate home country accepted in an address form;
- no work-auth question;
- no exclusion found.

Decisive negative:
- exact role limited to another country/region excluding the candidate's home country and configured eligible regions;
- required residence/work authorization there with no bridge;
- no-sponsorship language combined with foreign work-auth requirement;
- closed official country list excluding the candidate's home country.

Research is bounded. Exact-role official evidence beats general company information. Stop after decisive evidence. Normally max 2 authoritative sources. If still unclear, watchlist and move on.
